## 📖 What Is Mekanism?

**Mekanism** is an independent Minecraft add-on featuring high-tech machinery that can be used to create powerful tools, armor, and weapons. The mod features a tier-based system to organize the majority of its core features — Basic, Advanced, Elite, and Ultimate — which players can upgrade by crafting or using tier installers.

Every time you smelt an ore directly into its respective ingot, you're wasting the majority of what you've mined. Mekanism's advanced ore processing machinery extracts resources with utmost efficiency — you can now double, triple, quadruple, even **quintuple** your ingot production per ore.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Tier-based progression** — Basic → Advanced → Elite → Ultimate
- ✅ **5x ore processing** — Maximize resource yield
- ✅ **Factories** — Process up to 9 items simultaneously in one machine
- ✅ **Jetpacks** — Hydrogen-powered flight with multiple modes
- ✅ **Digital Miner** — Fully automated resource mining
- ✅ **Fusion Reactor** — Harness the power of the sun
- ✅ **Robit** — A cute robotic friend to follow you around
- ✅ **Transmitters** — Transfer items, fluids, gases, energy, and heat
